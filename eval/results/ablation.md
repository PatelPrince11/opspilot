# Phase 2 retrieval ablation (dev split, 100 questions)

**Rule: a difference is claimed only when its paired-bootstrap 95% CI excludes 0.** Paired bootstrap over dev questions, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261003. Everything else is reported but not claimed. Many comparisons are made, so expect about one in twenty null comparisons to exclude 0 by chance; a lone borderline CI is weak evidence.

Doc-level metrics: retrieve 30 chunks, dedupe to documents by best rank. all-gold R@10 counts questions with 2+ gold docs only (its n in brackets). Latency is retrieve() wall time on the Mac's CPU (M-series, models warm), one query at a time.
Generated by `python -m eval.ablation report` from the newest dev run of each config in eval_runs.

## Findings (hand-written; kept when the tables are regenerated)

Numbers below come from the tables above and from `python -m eval.diagnose_ablation` (dev only;
run with the chunk settings that are ingested). Nothing here touched the test split.

**1. Done-check failed: hybrid_rerank does not beat dense on exact-token Recall@5.**
+0.022 [-0.067, +0.122] at 400/50 and +0.044 [-0.033, +0.133] at 256/32 (n = 45). Neither CI
excludes 0, so no claim is made.

**2. Why the keyword leg is weak: `ts_rank_cd` has no IDF, and hostnames split into common
suffixes.** `ts_rank_cd` scores how often and how close the matched lexemes occur. Every
lexeme counts the same, however common it is. The punctuation split turns
`card-processor-proxy-01` into `card | processor | proxy | 01`, and `01` is in 245 of 547
chunks (`02` in 207). Chunks that repeat common host parts outrank chunks that hold the rare,
distinguishing tokens.
- Q003 (gold INC-0015): its best gold chunk matches 9 of 11 query lexemes, including the rare
  `february` (11 chunks). It still ranks 15th.
- Q027 (gold INC-0185, RB-027): `died` is in 1 chunk and `systemctl` in 14. The best gold chunk
  ranks 13th, behind chunks that repeat `01`, `02` and `restart`.

Keyword is below dense even on exact-token questions: -0.089 at 400/50, not claimed.
Diagnostic check: drop query lexemes found in more than 20% of chunks (a crude IDF cut). At
256/32, keyword exact-token Recall@5 goes from 0.744 to 0.844, above dense's 0.800. So the
missing IDF is the cause. The cut is a diagnostic, not the locked config.

**3. Why plain hybrid is worse than dense.** RRF gives both lists an equal vote, so the weak
keyword list pulls paraphrase questions down: -0.204 [-0.352, -0.056] at 400/50 and -0.148
[-0.259, -0.056] at 256/32 (both claimed). The reranker recovers that loss:
hybrid_rerank vs hybrid is +0.102 [+0.044, +0.163] at 400/50 and +0.054 [+0.003, +0.106] at
256/32 (both claimed).

**4. Why even a fixed keyword leg would not pass the check on this set: dense already finds the
token-bearing incident.**
- At 400/50, 14 of 45 exact-token questions have dense Recall@5 below 1. In 10 of them some
  gold doc is in the top 5, and in 13 the missing gold includes a runbook.
- Runbooks have no hostnames, codes or dates, so exact-token matching cannot find them.
  The token that does help pins the incident, and dense gets that incident anyway in a
  147-document corpus.
- That leaves dense at 0.800 on 45 questions. With the IDF cut, hybrid_rerank vs dense on
  exact-token is +0.056 [-0.022, +0.144]: still no claim.
- The textbook failure ("dense misses error codes") is small at this corpus size. Detecting a
  gain of about 5 points would need more exact-token questions than dev has.

**5. Where rerank does help: ordering.** On exact-token questions the gold doc is at rank 1 for
27/45 (dense) vs 38/45 (hybrid_rerank) at 400/50, and 28 vs 40 at 256/32.
- MRR difference, hybrid_rerank minus dense: +0.182 [+0.077, +0.290] on exact-token and
  +0.176 [+0.110, +0.244] overall at 400/50; +0.165 [+0.069, +0.265] and +0.139
  [+0.075, +0.204] at 256/32.
- MRR is a secondary metric. It does not replace the Recall@5 done-check, which failed.

**6. bge query prefix: no overall difference can be claimed** for any mode or chunk size. One
slice, hybrid @256/32 multi-doc, is +0.060 [+0.006, +0.125]. That is weak evidence among this
many comparisons. With rerank at 400/50 the prefix changes nothing: the fused top 30 hold
the same chunks, and the reranker re-orders them identically.
