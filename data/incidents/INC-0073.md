# Why logins returned 503 on 21 August 2025, in questions

sev3 · 2025-08-21 · 16:05 to 16:40 UTC (35 minutes) · auth-service against auth-db-01 · runbook: RB-024

**What was the user-visible effect?**

From 16:05 logins on `/v1/auth/login` came back `503`. Both pods, auth-service-01 and auth-service-02, could not set up new connections to auth-db-01, and the share of failing logins rose as time passed.

**Why a slow rise and not a cliff?**

Name checking happens at connect time. Pooled connections already open at 16:05 carried on until auth-service's pool let them go: `pool_max_conn_lifetime=1h`, `pool_max_conn_idle_time=30m`, so sooner for the idle ones. Each pod may hold up to 30 pooled connections (`pool_max_conns`), 2 x 30 = 60 across the two pods, and every one that retired could only be replaced by a connection that would be rejected.

**What did the certificate lack?**

auth-service uses `sslmode=verify-full` against the internal CA, which includes a match between the certificate and the name being dialled. The server certificate on auth-db-01 did not carry the name auth-service checks. The names it did contain are not recorded.

**Could it be a repeat of an earlier certificate incident?**

No. It was not an expiry and not a renewed certificate that Postgres had failed to load, which was the cause on 2025-04-08. RB-024 is cited here for the auth-db certificate arrangement.

**Who handled it, and when did the errors stop?**

team-dba took the incident, and logins were normal by 16:40. What ended the failures was not written down.

**How long was it?**

The incident record runs from 16:05 to 16:40, which is 35 minutes. The pool retirement described above is why the failure rate was not constant across those 35 minutes. No count of failed logins exists, which leaves the scale of the impact open. Logins that did succeed in the same window did so on connections that predated the certificate problem.

**What remains unanswered?**

- Which names the auth-db-01 certificate carries, compared with the name auth-service dials.
- How a certificate without the right name came to be installed.
- Whether every older connection had retired by 16:40, or whether some still worked when the errors stopped.
