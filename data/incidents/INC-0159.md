# Handoff: card processor pushback, Tuesday 19 May 2026

**card-processor-proxy-01, card-processor-proxy-02 · CPX-W429 · sev3 · 06:55 to 07:05 UTC · no runbook · closed**

Passing this on closed. Both proxies have stopped logging `CPX-W429` and payments are going through to the processor again.

## What happened

At 06:55 the two proxy pods began logging `CPX-W429`, which our catalog describes as the processor's rate limit being hit, with the request delayed or rejected. Both pods logged it together, so the pushback arrived on each proxy's calls to the processor rather than from a fault in one of our pods. It lasted ten minutes, until 07:05, and then the code stopped appearing.

Our own retries were not driving it: each payment kept its usual three attempts and no more, so no extra load from our side was feeding the pushback.

## Scope

card-processor-proxy runs as two pods, and card payments from payments-api reach the processor through them. The processor applies a limit of 400 requests per second to our merchant account. The request rate during these ten minutes was not recorded, so it cannot be said how close to that figure the traffic came. Both proxies saw the code, so payments through either proxy were exposed while it lasted.

## Who has it

The payments on-call holds the incident, and the severity stays at sev3.

## Loose ends

- How many payments failed during the ten minutes was not counted.
- Why the processor pushed back was not passed to us.
