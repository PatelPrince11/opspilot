# Wednesday 16 July 2025: 504s that traced back to a node

sev3 · 05:50 to 06:35 UTC · cause on the edge's node · runbook: RB-014

**What went wrong, from the outside?**

From 05:50 requests passing through edge-gateway-03 came back as `504`. More than one route was affected, so this was not a single endpoint misbehaving. The window ran 45 minutes, to 06:35.

**Why did we not start with a service team?**

Because the errors were not tied to one route. When a single service is slow, its route shows the `504`s and its owners get the call. Here the common element was the gateway pod that carried the requests, so the useful question was what that pod shared with everything it served.

**What did RB-014 say about that?**

It treats latency that appears everywhere at once as a shared cause, one of which is pressure on the node pool, and routes it to team-platform. That was the entry that fit: the cause sat on the node running the edge, not in any upstream service.

**How long did it last, and what changed in between?**

The incident opened at 05:50 and was closed at 06:35, 45 minutes later. The record does not hold a mid-incident change on the pods, the routes or the upstream services; the shape is a start, a hand-off to the owning team, and an end.

**Who took it?**

team-platform, as the runbook directs for shared causes. The 504s had stopped by 06:35.

**Was the pod itself the problem?**

The evidence we have places the problem on the node under edge-gateway-03, and the pod was the place where it showed up. We did not record a node-level message or a metric that proves how the pressure arose, and we are not claiming one.

**Why was a restart of the pod not the answer we recorded?**

Nothing here says a restart was tried or helped. The runbook's shared-cause entry points to the node pool and its owner, so that is where the ticket went.

**Open items**

- Record, next time, which signal on the node first showed the pressure, so the pod does not have to be the thing we reason from.
- Keep `504` on several routes at once as the cue to look below the services.
