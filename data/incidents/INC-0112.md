# Handoff: catalog-api release delayed by its migration (22 December 2025)

**catalog-api-02, catalog-db-01 · release delayed · sev4 · 20:37 to 21:42 UTC · RB-028 · closed**

## Where this stands

Closed at 21:42, after 65 minutes. The catalog-api release did not go out on time: it was delayed for the whole incident. How it proceeded afterwards is not recorded.

## What happened

The release carried a database migration. A Job is a one-off task, while the Deployment is the long-running set of catalog-api pods, and migrations are not part of those pods; they run in a pre-deploy Job, `catalog-api-migrate`, and the Deployment only moves once that Job has finished successfully. Since 2025-05-16 the Job sets `PGOPTIONS='-c lock_timeout=5s'`, so a migration waits at most 5 s for a lock before it gives up.

From 20:37 the migration needed a lock on catalog-db-01 that something else was holding. It hit the 5 s limit, the Job failed and retried, and as long as the failures went on the Deployment stayed put and the release waited. catalog-api-02 is the pod named in the logs next to the Job's failures. RB-028 is the runbook for migration locks.

## Effect

The cost was the delay itself: a release that was late by the length of the incident. It is rated sev4 and carries no customer-facing error code.

## Unknowns

What held the lock on catalog-db-01 was not recorded, and neither was the number of attempts or what ended the failures.

## Next

Whoever finds the lock holder should note it in the ticket; this time it was not noted.
