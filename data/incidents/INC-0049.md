# Reservations waiting on row locks in inventory-db-01

sev2 · 2025-06-04 · 15:55 to 17:30 UTC · affected: inventory-service, orders-api · runbook: RB-015

| Who or what | UTC | Seen |
|---|---|---|
| inventory-service-01, inventory-service-02 | 15:55 | Stock reservations time out on both instances, each logged as `INV-E220`. |
| orders-api | during the window | Checkout depends on the stock check, so orders-api is where customers feel the delay. It is a symptom service here; the inventory code is the one that identifies the incident. |
| inventory-db-01 | during the window | The sessions showed lock waits on the `reservations` table. The holder is not known. |
| inventory squad | 17:30 | `INV-E220` stopped on both instances. Closed. |

## Impact and cause

RB-015 is the card cited for slow stock reservations; this incident falls in its lock-wait part. A reservation writes to `reservations`, and a write to a row that another transaction holds a lock on cannot proceed until that transaction ends. While the lock lasted, the writes queued behind it, and each one eventually ran past the service's timeout and was logged as `INV-E220`.

The service side explains how widely it spread. inventory-service uses a pool of 20 connections per instance, so the two instances together hold 2 x 20 = 40 connections. While lock waits lasted, those connections could be taken by requests that were waiting, and new reservations queued behind them.

## What this was not

It was not a missing index. Nor was it a shortage of connection slots: the service's 40 connections are well under inventory-db's 97 usable ones (100 minus 3).

## Open points

- Which session held the locks, and what it was doing, was not recorded.
- Whether the locks came from a release, a manual session or a batch was not recorded.
- What released them was not recorded. The inventory squad handled it, and `INV-E220` was gone by 17:30.

## Follow-ups

- Record the blocking session's details as soon as lock waits appear, since they are gone once the lock is released.
- Add a lock-wait panel for inventory-db-01 to the dashboard the squad opens first.
