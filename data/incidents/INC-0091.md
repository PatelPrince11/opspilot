# The Wednesday evening catalog-api could not get a connection

On 15 October 2025, from 21:30 until 22:10 UTC, product requests to catalog-api that needed the database stopped coming back. The incident is rated sev3. It lasted 40 minutes, and the instances involved were catalog-api-01 and catalog-api-02.

Product requests that needed catalog-db were the ones affected. For those, the edge gave up with a `504` once its 15-second route timeout ran out.

## How the pools work, and why they filled

Each catalog-api instance uses a SQLAlchemy QueuePool of 10 connections, with room to overflow by 5 more. That is 15 per instance, and with two instances 2 x 15 = 30 connections for the whole service. The pool is a queue: a request that finds all 15 in use waits for one to come back, and after 30 seconds it gives up with the pool's own timeout error, which says the limit of size 10 with overflow 5 was reached.

That 30-second wait is longer than the edge's 15-second route timeout. So a request that waited for a connection typically drew a `504` from the edge at 15 seconds, and the pool timeout followed in the service log about 15 seconds later (30 - 15), after the edge had already answered.

What kept the connections busy was slow work on catalog-db. A query that normally returns quickly took long enough that each connection stayed checked out far beyond its usual short stay. With 30 connections in total, it took only a modest number of those slow queries running together to use up the whole supply. The query was not recorded, and neither was the reason it was slow.

## What people did

The edge alerts on its `504` rate. RB-028 is the runbook for catalog-api pool trouble, so it was the one cited. It is about a migration waiting on a lock, and nothing in this incident was a migration.

The catalog on-call took the incident. The pool errors and the `504`s had stopped by 22:10, when it was closed. What ended the slow query was not recorded.

## What stays open

- The identity of the slow query and the cause of its slowness.
- Whether the 30-second pool wait should stay longer than the edge's 15 seconds. 
- An alert on pool wait time per instance, ahead of the edge's `504` rate.
