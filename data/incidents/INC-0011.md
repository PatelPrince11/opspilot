# orders-db-01: journal growth on /var (sev4)

Tuesday 4 February 2025, 03:05 to 05:05 UTC, 120 minutes. Runbook RB-008. At 03:05 I saw the 90% `/var` alert for orders-db-01, and the next two readings were higher still. Because `/var` shares the root disk on our VMs, a full `/var` would have meant a full root filesystem for the database host, so I dealt with it then rather than leaving it for the morning. While I worked, orders-db answered queries normally and orders-api showed no errors.

The space had gone into `/var/log/journal`, and `journalctl --disk-usage` agreed. A vacuum to 500M brought `/var` well under the threshold straight away. To keep it there I put `SystemMaxUse=500M` into `/etc/systemd/journald.conf`, restarted journald, and stayed with the graph until 05:05, when it had been level for long enough to close.
