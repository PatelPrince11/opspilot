# Short pushback from the card processor

sev4 · 2026-09-12 · 00:30 to 00:35 UTC · cause: card processor · affected: payments-api · runbook: RB-029

| Host or team | UTC | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| card-processor-proxy-01 | 00:30 | Began relaying the processor's own rate-limit reply and logging `CPX-W429`. |
| card-processor-proxy-02 | 00:30 | The same, starting in the same minute. |
| payments-api | 00:30 | A payment whose 3 attempts all met the pushback ended in `PAYAPI-E1107`. Those payments failed for the customer. |
| both proxies | 00:35 | `CPX-W429` stopped. Payments went through normally again. |

## Effect on payments

For five minutes some card payments were declined at the last step. The processor sat behind both proxies, so the pushback reached payments-api through either one, and the payments that failed were those whose every attempt landed in the spell.

## What marked it as the processor's doing

The proxy has its own limiter, and what it rejects never leaves the proxy. `CPX-W429` is different: it is logged when the processor itself answers with its rate-limit reply. That was the only code on the proxy side in these five minutes, and both pods wrote it from the same minute. Nothing here pointed at one instance being gone.

## Retries did not drive it

The checkout path can repeat a failed call, and in a bad spell the repeats can keep the processor pushing back. The record shows nothing like that here. The spell began abruptly at 00:30 and ended at 00:35, and no change to the attempt setting is recorded. Each affected payment used its 3 attempts and then failed.

## Open points

What made the processor push back at 00:30 was not recorded, and neither was the request rate toward it in those minutes. The processor made no statement that we hold.

## Afterwards

No change on our side is recorded, and the incident stayed at sev4. If a pushback of this kind returns, the first thing to settle is whether the code on the proxies is the processor's or the proxy's own; the two lead to different owners.
