# Egress limiter rejections on both proxies, 2026-08-13

payments timeline, sev1, 21:52 to 22:07 UTC. Runbook used: RB-029.

```
21:52  card-processor-proxy-01 and card-processor-proxy-02 both begin to log CPX-W430
21:52  payments-api starts logging PAYAPI-E1107 for payments whose 3 attempts were all refused
22:07  CPX-W430 gone from both proxies; PAYAPI-E1107 back to its usual level
```

## Impact

For 15 minutes a card payment could end in failure whenever its attempts met an empty bucket. Some attempts that waited for a token still went through, only later than usual. The rest were refused and customers saw a failed payment.

## The first minutes

Card payments started to fail at 21:52, and the sev1 clock starts there. The proxy lines carried our own limiter's code, so the refusals were made inside the proxies and the attempts never travelled on to the processor. Because both pods logged it in the same minute, no single instance was missing; RB-029 reads that pattern as demand above the combined budget.

## The size of the budget

Each pod admits 180 attempts per second, 2 x 180 = 360 for the pair, and retries count as attempts. An attempt that cannot get a token within 2 s is dropped. A payment is reported as failed once all 3 of its attempts have been dropped, which is what payments-api recorded with `PAYAPI-E1107`.

## Gaps

The payment request rate for the window was not recorded, so there is no figure to set against 360. Why traffic toward the processor was high at 21:52 was not recorded either.

## Handling

The payments on-call took the incident. No limiter setting or attempt count change is recorded. The rejections had stopped by 22:07, 15 minutes after they began.

## Follow-ups

- Put the proxy rejection count and the payments-api attempt count on one panel, so an excess over the budget can be read directly.
- Trace where the extra traffic came from, starting from the 21:52 mark.
