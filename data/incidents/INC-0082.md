# Abandoned handshakes on edge-gateway-03, 21 September 2025

Service edge-gateway, sev4, from 10:45 to 15:05 UTC (260 minutes). Runbook: RB-025.

```
10:45  handshakes from one client start being abandoned; edge-gateway-03 logs them
15:05  closed; the cause was the client's own device clock, and the edge stays as it was
```

Only two moments are on record, so the timeline has two entries. The 260 minutes run from the first abandoned handshake to the close, which is four hours and twenty minutes.

## Where the log lines came from

edge-gateway-03 wrote the log lines for the broken-off handshakes, and its certificate was examined. A wrong clock fails against every pod the client reaches, so the pod that holds the log says nothing about the route the client took.

## Cause and decision

The clock on the client's device was wrong, so the client judged our certificate against the wrong date. RB-025 sets that case apart from faults in what the edge serves: one caller affected, others fine. After the examination of the certificate on edge-gateway-03, the edge was left alone. No certificate file, Secret or pod was touched, and the incident ended at 15:05 with nothing changed on our side.

## The edge as it stood

Callers reach the gateway on port 443. Its certificate comes from a public CA, lives in a Secret and is mounted as files into every pod; Envoy reads the files at start, so a renewal needs a restart of the Deployment. None of that was touched during the 260 minutes, and the incident record names no change to any of it.

## Scale

The incident carried sev4. One caller is on record as affected, and no count of abandoned handshakes was kept; RB-025 describes this case as one client failing while the others connect.

## Unrecorded

Who the caller was, which device it used and what error it showed. No reading of the device clock was taken either.
