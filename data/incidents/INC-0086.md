# Handoff, 6 October 2025: edge chain served incomplete

**edge-gateway, every pod of the three. sev3, resolved. 18:20 to 19:25 UTC (65 minutes). Runbook: RB-025.**

State at handover: closed, nothing running against the live edge.

The served certificate file lacked the intermediate. It held the certificate for our public name and stopped there. A client that already has the intermediate cached can still complete the chain; one that does not has no link from our certificate to the public CA and drops the connection. That split is why this was sev3: traffic dipped without disappearing.

Sequence:

- 18:20 · traffic through the edge began to dip.
- Diagnosis · an `openssl s_client` handshake to each pod in turn (edge-gateway-01, then -02, then -03) printed `Verify return code: 21 (unable to verify the first certificate)` every time. Same result from all three pointed at the shared file rather than one pod, and 21 is RB-025's marker for an absent intermediate. A lapse would have shown 10.
- 19:25 · handshakes succeeding and traffic at its normal level again. Closed.

Not recorded: why the intermediate was missing from the file, and what ended the failures. RB-025 gives its own remedy for this row (our certificate followed by the intermediate, both in the one file); no entry says that is what was done. No count of affected connections exists.

Passing on:

- Whoever owns certificate renewal should be asked how the file came to lack the intermediate.
- Consider extending the daily outside probe from "expiry date" to "chain completes".
