# internal-dns-01 silent: slow lookups from VMs (sev3)

3 February 2026, 07:20 to 08:05 UTC. Runbook RB-020. From 07:20 internal-dns-01 did not answer queries. Names kept resolving, just late. VMs put internal-dns-01 ahead of the other server in their resolver list, so each lookup that reached the resolver paid the timeout, 5 s per attempt with 2 attempts, before the other server replied. kafka-02 was the host where the delay was visible. Calls that rely on redis-cache were slow as well, though no host of theirs is named here.

The fault sat in the server, with the zone not in question. internal-dns-01 was brought back to answering, and by 08:05 lookups from kafka-02 had returned to normal speed. The cause of the silence is not established in this write-up.
