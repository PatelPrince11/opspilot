# Handoff: ledger-service CPU, Thursday 23 October 2025

**ledger-service-01 and ledger-service-02 · CPU high with LEDGER-E455 in the logs · sev2 · 16:40 to 17:55 UTC · RB-001 · closed**

Short version: Kafka stopped accepting our publishes, and ledger-service's retry threads kept hammering a queue that could not drain. The CPU alarm was a side effect; the trigger was upstream of us. It ended when the outage did.

## State you inherit

- Both ledger-service-01 and ledger-service-02 are healthy and `LEDGER-E455` has stopped.
- Publishes to the ledger topic succeed and the retry queue is empty. CPU on both instances is back to its usual level.

## What the incident looked like

From 16:40 both instances ran hot together, which pointed away from a single-instance cause. Several `ledger-retry-*` threads were busy in their retry loop, and each entry that could not be published produced a `LEDGER-E455` line before going back on the queue. That is the retry spin: the worker polls again immediately instead of waiting, so CPU climbs for as long as publishes keep failing. RB-001 was my guide for telling it apart from garbage collection and from a heavy batch.

The way out was on the Kafka side. Once publishes were accepted again, the queue drained by itself and the threads settled. By 17:55 it was over.

## What I do not have

I have no cause for the Kafka refusal. Ask whoever owns that side.

## For the next shift

- The retry worker should pause between attempts, with the delay growing, rather than polling flat out. A Kafka outage can cause this again while the worker polls flat out.
- If asked for evidence, the thread dumps from both pods show the retry threads in the same loop.
