# Postmortem: a slow processor and a checkout path that kept asking

Date 2026-05-26. Window 17:05 to 17:50 UTC. Severity sev1. Proxy hosts involved: card-processor-proxy-02 and card-processor-proxy-01. Reference consulted: RB-014.

## Impact

For the length of the window customers trying to pay at checkout got an error page from the edge: `504` for about 40 mins. Behind it, payments-api logged `PAYAPI-E1107` for payments that ran out of attempts, orders-api logged `ORD-5003` for calls that failed or timed out from its side, and the proxies recorded `CPX-W429` each time the processor pushed back on one of our calls.

## What we saw, and in what order

Around 17:05 the card processor slowed down and began answering with its rate-limit reply. The first visible signs were on the proxies, with `CPX-W429` on both. The checkout path above them has two places that retry a failed call, and each is set to 3 attempts, so a single checkout could turn into as many as 9 calls toward the processor. As the processor got slower, those repeats arrived on top of the original traffic.

The page came from the edge. The on-call opened RB-014 and followed its `/checkout` row to orders-api. That was correct but one hop short of the cause: orders-api was waiting on payments-api, and payments-api on the proxy.

## Who saw what

| Team or layer | What it saw |
|---|---|
| Edge | `504` on checkout for most of the window |
| orders-api | `ORD-5003` as its payment calls failed or timed out |
| payments-api | `PAYAPI-E1107` once a payment used all its attempts |
| Proxies | `CPX-W429` replies relayed from the processor |

The processor itself allows 400 requests a second for our merchant account. Each layer saw a different symptom of the same slow dependency.

The whole window ran 45 minutes, from 17:05 to 17:50.

## Decision and result

The retries were the part we controlled. payments-api was switched to CPX_MAX_ATTEMPTS=1 so that each payment made one call toward the processor. orders-api was left as it was. Within minutes the `CPX-W429` and `PAYAPI-E1107` volume fell and the edge stopped returning `504`, and the incident closed at 17:50.

## What we do not know

The reason for the processor's slowdown is not known to us, and we have no figures for how the request rate at each hop compared during the window.

## Follow-ups

1. Put the call rate of each hop on one dashboard so the amplification can be seen directly.
2. Decide whether the checkout path should share a retry allowance between its layers.
3. Write down in the on-call notes where each layer's attempt setting lives, so the first responder can find them without searching.
4. Note in the on-call guide that a slowing processor shows up first as `CPX-W429` on the proxies.
