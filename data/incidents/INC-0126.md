# Handoff: logins refused on Sunday 8 February 2026

**Closed. sev2, 09:00 to 09:55 UTC, 55 minutes. Hosts: auth-db-01, auth-service-01, auth-service-02. Runbook: RB-024.**

## State at close

The incident was closed at 09:55 with logins normal. team-dba handled it.

## The parts involved

auth-db-01 is the primary database for authentication. auth-service, which runs as two pods here (auth-service-01 and auth-service-02), reaches it as the role `auth_app` and sets `sslmode=verify-full`. A login that cannot obtain a database connection is answered with `503`.

## What happened

From 09:00 every connection either pod tried to open was refused during the TLS handshake. `verify-full` needs the server's certificate to list the name the client connected to, and the primary database's certificate did not. Logins that depended on a fresh connection returned `503` until the incident closed.

RB-024 is the runbook for these certificates, but neither an expiry nor a renewal that was never loaded applies here.
