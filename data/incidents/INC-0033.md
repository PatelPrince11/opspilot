## Why it alternated

auth-service-01 resolves through the cluster CoreDNS. For each forwarded `prod.internal` query that resolver picks a server by chance, and it caches whatever returns for 30 seconds at most. Every cache refill was thus a coin toss between the intact server and internal-dns-02: the address from one, `NXDOMAIN` from the other, kept for as long as half a minute.

## Outcome

Handled by team-platform; errors gone by 01:40. The step that ended them is not recorded, and neither is how the record dropped out of the pushed file. RB-020 gives a remedy for a record missing from a zone. Nothing on this board says it was the step taken.

## Scope

Only auth-service-01 appears in the record. The number of failed attempts is not recorded. The 25 minutes are the span from 01:15 to 01:40.
