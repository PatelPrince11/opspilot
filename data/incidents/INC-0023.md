# Digest of the #platform-oncall thread, Sunday 16 March 2025

sev3, 10:15 to 11:15 UTC. Summarised by role, oldest first. Runbook referred to: RB-025.

**10:31 support:** a partner integration reports TLS failures on about a third of its calls since around 10:15. Browsers show certificate warnings for some customers, not all.

**10:38 platform on-call:** request rate for edge-gateway-01 has been close to zero since 10:15 and the other two pods picked up the difference. The access logs show nothing wrong, as expected: a client that rejects our certificate never sends a request.

**10:45 platform on-call:** going through RB-025's list of certificate failures. Partial failure across many clients points at one pod rather than the clients' clocks.

**10:52 platform on-call:** `openssl s_client` with `-servername` against each pod. edge-gateway-01 presents a certificate whose notAfter is 2025-03-16 10:15 UTC. The other two present the renewed one. One pod missed the renewal.

**10:55 platform lead:** how? The certificate was renewed into the Secret on 2025-02-14, 30 days ahead. The renewal note at the time said to restart every edge pod, and whoever did it used `kubectl delete pod`, one at a time, waiting for each replacement to be Ready so two pods kept serving. They picked each name from a fresh pod list. The third time, the name at the top was the replacement created by the first delete, so that pod was restarted twice and the original pod behind edge-gateway-01 never was. Envoy reads the certificate only at start, so it kept the old one in memory for a month.

**10:56 platform lead:** and the daily outside probe kept reporting the new date, because it went through the load balancer and reached one of the renewed pods.

**10:58 platform on-call:** `rollout restart` on the edge-gateway Deployment. It brings up a replacement before retiring each old pod, so capacity never dropped below three.

**11:06 platform on-call:** every pod presents the renewed expiry date.

**11:15 platform on-call:** request rates look normal again on every pod. Closing.

**Agreed follow-ups:** renewals use `rollout restart`, never hand-deleted pods; after any renewal, check each pod with `s_client`, not just the public name; make the outside probe test each pod.
