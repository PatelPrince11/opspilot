# Questions and answers: the redis-cache failover of 19 June 2025

sev4. 02:25 to 02:40 UTC on 19 June 2025, 15 minutes. Hosts: redis-cache-01 and redis-cache-02. Runbook: RB-017.

**Was anything broken?**

No fault caused this. The primary of redis-cache, the master called `cache`, was handed over from redis-cache-01 to redis-cache-02 by choice. `SENTINEL FAILOVER <master name>` is the documented way to ask for such a move. The record does not say why the move was planned, or who ran it.

**What did customers see?**

catalog-api logged `CAT-E301` for under a minute from 02:25. That code means a product read could not be served from the cache and was answered from catalog-db instead, so those reads took the slower path. How many reads were affected was not recorded.

**Why does a brief CAT-E301 matter?**

RB-017 is written to protect catalog-db when the cache goes away, because a read that misses the cache becomes a query on the database. No database trouble is recorded for this incident.

**What is redis-cache made of?**

It is Redis 7 with one primary and one replica, redis-cache-01 and redis-cache-02, and Sentinel manages which of them is primary. The master name `cache` is how Sentinel refers to the pair. Before the move the primary was redis-cache-01; afterwards it was redis-cache-02.

**Was it an automatic failover?**

No. An automatic failover happens when Sentinel judges the primary unreachable. Here the primary was healthy and the handover was a deliberate act.

**Which hosts took part?**

redis-cache-01 held the primary role until the move and redis-cache-02 held it afterwards. Both appear in the logs around the move.

**What became of redis-cache-01?**

Its state after the handover is not recorded. Three Sentinel processes watch the `cache` master with a quorum of 2, and any of them can answer that question. The current primary can be read from Sentinel with `SENTINEL get-master-addr-by-name cache`, a query given in RB-017.

**When was it over?**

By 02:40 catalog-api was reading from the cache again. The 15 minutes run from the first entry at 02:25 to that point, while the `CAT-E301` lines themselves lasted under a minute.

**Who handled it?**

team-platform took the incident.
