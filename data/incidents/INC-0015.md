# Processor answering slowly, Thursday 20 February 2025 (sev3)

Between 10:05 and 10:45 UTC our external processor took far longer than usual to reply. Both
egress instances, card-processor-proxy-01 alongside -02, measured identical slowness, which rules
out any single instance of ours. When every try at a payment either errored or timed out,
payments-api recorded `PAYAPI-E1107`.

What we can show is latency measured from our end; the processor has given us
no explanation of its own. Replies returned to their usual speed at 10:45,
without any intervention from us, and errors ended alongside.

Gap worth closing: there is no runbook for an upstream processor that turns slow. A short one
should cover where to look first and how to separate processor delay from trouble inside our own
egress layer.
