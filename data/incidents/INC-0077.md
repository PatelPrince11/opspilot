# 8 September 2025: ledger-service-01 and a pool that would not empty

Running log of a Monday morning. Rated sev2, times in UTC. Window 10:05 to 11:35, which is 90 minutes. Runbooks cited: RB-001 and RB-012.

## Reading the log

LEDGER-W210 means the Hikari pool on ledger-service-01 is more than 90% in use, and its limit is 20 connections. Once all 20 were checked out by calls waiting on ledger-db-01, the next caller queued. After 30 seconds Hikari gave up with its message that the request timed out after 30000ms, and payments-api received a 503 from the ledger. The chain is slow database work, a full pool, timed-out callers, and a 503 upstream.

RB-001 was the first runbook opened because LEDGER-W210 usually arrives ahead of a 503, and the card warns that this ordering tempts people to treat it as a database problem when it may be the JVM. Here the slow queries on ledger-db-01 were the holders of the connections. RB-012 supplied the budget: ledger-service's two pods hold 2 x 20 = 40 of the 197 usable slots on ledger-db (200 minus 3), so a bigger pool was not a remedy.

## Carried forward

- Alert when callers start waiting for a connection (pending above 0).
- When LEDGER-W210 fires, look at the database sessions before the JVM.
