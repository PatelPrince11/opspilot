# Catalog edge errors on 15 May 2025: the connection count was not the cause

catalog on-call write-up, sev3, 14:02 to 14:09 UTC. Runbook consulted: RB-012.

## What customers and the edge saw

Pages served from cache kept loading. Misses and writes were the casualties: edge-gateway began answering 504 from 14:02:15, and the 504-rate alert paged catalog on-call at 14:06. The errors ended with the 14:09 cancel of the schema change, and the 504s were gone within the minute that followed.

## The question that mattered at 14:07

A full pool on catalog-api usually sends people straight to the database's connection limit, which is what RB-012 covers. So the first check on catalog-db-01 was the session count: 32 sessions against 97 usable slots, CPU low. That is far from the ceiling, which ruled the limit out and moved attention to what the sessions were waiting on.

## Rough size of the stall

Roughly 60 misses and 5 writes reach the database each second, about 65 requests a second. A stalled request keeps its pooled connection, and the pods hold only 30 between them, so the pool drained almost immediately. catalog-api-01 and catalog-api-02 began logging QueuePool timeouts at 14:02:30, which fits requests that had started waiting for a connection thirty seconds earlier.

The numbers point to waiting, rather than work, as what filled the pool.

## Follow-up

- Run migrations with a short `lock_timeout` so a blocked one gives up quickly; this took effect from 2025-05-16.
