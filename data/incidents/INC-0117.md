# Edge 504s after a route configuration change (sev3)

Friday 9 January 2026, 11:45 to 12:25 UTC, 40 minutes. Runbook: RB-014. All three edge pods were involved: edge-gateway-02, edge-gateway-01 and edge-gateway-03.

A change to the route configuration reached the edge, and from 11:45 the gateway began returning `504` to callers. The pods carried the same change, so the symptom was not tied to a single pod. RB-014 is the triage table for slow requests at the edge and reads a `504` as an upstream that did not finish within the 15 second route timeout.

Little else about the change is on record. Which route was edited, which setting in it moved and who made the edit were not written down, and neither were the split of failures between routes nor the count of failed requests. The change is the cause because the `504`s began when it arrived; the record offers no further test of that.

team-platform handled it; the 504s had stopped by 12:25.
