# Card processor pushed back for four minutes (sev4)

On 2025-06-02, between 22:05 and 22:10 UTC, the card processor turned away part of our traffic with its rate-limit response. card-processor-proxy-01 and card-processor-proxy-02 both logged `CPX-W429` from 22:05:20 to 22:09:10, and about 180 payments failed with `PAYAPI-E1107`. Customers saw a declined payment.

We were sending about 100 payment requests a second, a quarter of the 400 per second the processor allows our merchant account, so this was not our traffic hitting the limit. The processor reported a fault on its side. Nothing was changed on ours.

We have no runbook for the processor pushing back. If it happens again, write one.
