# Thursday 7 August 2025: the edge stopped being trusted at 04:10

The outage ran from 04:10 to 06:55 UTC on 7 August 2025, which is 165 minutes, and it was a sev1. I was on the team-platform rota, and this is the sequence as it reached me.

What came in first was silence rather than errors. Request rates on every route of edge-gateway fell together at 04:10 and stayed down. Pod 01, pod 02 and pod 03 (edge-gateway-01, edge-gateway-02, edge-gateway-03) all behaved alike, so a single sick pod was ruled out from the first graph. The access logs were quiet too: clients were leaving during the handshake, before Envoy had a request to log.

RB-025 is the reference for the edge certificate. I ran `openssl s_client` against each pod, naming the public host with `-servername`. All three printed `Verify return code: 10 (certificate has expired)`. That code alone suggests a lapsed certificate for our own name. It was not that. Printing the whole chain with `-showcerts` showed the past date sitting on the intermediate the pods hand out, while the certificate for our own name had not run out.

That changed what would help. Renewing our own certificate and restarting the pods would have left clients facing the same dead intermediate. RB-025's fix for this row is to fetch the certificate authority's current chain.

team-platform kept the incident until the end, and by 06:55 handshakes were succeeding again and request rates were back to their usual shape. Which steps were taken is not recorded, and neither is why the expiry of the intermediate was not caught beforehand.

All three pods were affected equally, since each serves the same files, so the load balancer's even spread of connections gave no relief: any connection landed on a pod presenting the same chain. No share of clients and no count of failed connections was kept.

What I would carry forward:

- After a certificate event, read every certificate in the chain a pod serves, not only the one carrying our name.
- A fall in request rates with an empty access log is a handshake problem until shown otherwise.
