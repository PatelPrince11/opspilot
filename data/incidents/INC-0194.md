## What made it short

The page came six minutes after the first errors, and the release was the obvious suspect: the
`503`s began with it, and there was no other change to look at. Rolling back needed no
diagnosis of the cause, only the decision to undo it. Finding the actual bug can happen after
the fact, without customers waiting.
