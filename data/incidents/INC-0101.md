# Handoff: night to day, 2025-11-18

**search-cluster-02, /var full. sev3, resolved. 05:30 to 06:40 UTC. Runbook: RB-008.**

Short version: one log file ate the root disk. Search was never affected because OpenSearch data lives on its own volume, `/data/opensearch`. What we lost is OpenSearch's own log lines on -02 between 05:30 and 06:10.

What I saw and did:

- 05:36 · paged by the "logs missing" alert for search-cluster-02. The only fresh lines were from the log agent itself, which ships its own errors over the network: `ENOSPC` writing its position file.
- 05:41 · `df -h /var`: 100% of the 40 GB root disk.
- 05:48 · `du -xh --max-depth=2 /var | sort -h`: one `*.log` file under `/var/log/opensearch/` at about 29 GB. Everything else normal.
- 05:52 · no OpenSearch entry in `/etc/logrotate.d/`. That's the RB-008 row for an unrotated app log, `/var/log/opensearch/*.log`.
- 06:02 · put the logrotate file back from config management (with `copytruncate`, because OpenSearch keeps the file open).
- 06:04 · `logrotate -f` on it. `/var` back to 11 GB, about 28%.
- 06:10 · OpenSearch lines arriving again.
- 06:40 · agent backlog shipped, nothing else growing. Closed.

Why it took this long to fill: -02 was rebuilt on 20 October 2025 and the rebuild never laid down the logrotate file. The log grows about 1 GB a day. From 20 October to 18 November is 29 days (11 left in October plus 18 in November), so about 29 GB of log on top of the usual 11 GB: 40 GB, the whole disk.

It wasn't silent. The `/var` warning at 90% (36 GB, so 25 GB of log, 25 days in) fired on 14 November around 05:30. That alert only opens a ticket, and nobody picked it up before the disk filled.

For day shift:

1. Config management: find out why rebuilt search-cluster nodes miss the OpenSearch logrotate file and check -01 and -03.
2. The 90% `/var` ticket needs an owner who reads it within a day.
3. If anyone needs OpenSearch logs from -02 for 05:30 to 06:10, there aren't any.
