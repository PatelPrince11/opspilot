# Ticket: certificate warning from one client, edge certificate healthy

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Severity | sev4 |
| Service | edge-gateway |

**Description.** One client says the edge's certificate is not accepted. The connections it makes end up on edge-gateway-03. No other client has complained.

## Comments

**13:40** · support: ticket opened from the client's report. The client sees a certificate error as soon as it connects, before any page or API response arrives.

**13:52** · platform on-call: an expired certificate was the first idea, since that is what the error text suggests. If the edge's own certificate had lapsed, many clients would be failing together. Only one has written in, so the edge's certificate is not the first suspect.

**14:10** · platform on-call: checked what edge-gateway-03 presents to a connecting client by opening a TLS connection to it and reading the certificate dates and chain. The certificate is in date and the chain is complete.

**14:25** · platform on-call: with the edge presenting a good certificate, the remaining candidate is the machine on the other end. One client failing while the rest of our traffic is untouched fits a device whose idea of today's date is wrong; a clock far from the real time makes a good certificate look expired or not yet valid. This is a client matter, and the edge has nothing to change.

**14:40** · platform on-call: reply sent to support with the findings and a suggestion for the client to check the date and time on the device and try again.

**15:05** · support: passed the advice on to the client in plain language.

**15:25** · platform on-call: closing as a client-side fault. No restart, no change to the Secret and no rollout on the edge.

## Scope of what is recorded

This ticket does not record the client's name, the make of its device or what exactly it showed on screen; none of that was needed to reach the conclusion. The only host that appears in the ticket is edge-gateway-03, the pod the client's connections reached.

## Why the severity stayed low

The report concerned a single client and the edge itself was healthy for everyone else, which is why this was handled at sev4 and run through support rather than as an outage. The ticket was open for 105 minutes (13:40 to 15:25).

## What was deliberately not done

The Secret `edge-public-tls` was left alone and no restart was made. Nothing on the server side was broken, and a restart would only have replaced pods for no reason. Renewing early would not have helped either, because the certificate the edge serves is already valid.

## Signs that point to the client next time

- One client is affected, and other clients are not failing.
- The certificate returned by a direct connection to the pod is in date and has its full chain.
- The edge's request volume has not changed.

When all three hold, the fix belongs with the client.
