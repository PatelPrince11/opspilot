## Why the alert threshold matters

On this volume the free space is about 95 GB on a normal day, while the server's own cap on slot WAL is 200 GB. A stuck slot would therefore fill the disk before the cap acted, which makes the 50 GB alert the working guard. It did its job here: the disk never filled and no out-of-space failure occurred.

## Who was touched

| Component | Effect |
|---|---|
| payments-db-01 | Carried extra WAL from 04:10 to 07:30 but kept running. |
| ledger reconciliation pipeline | The feed behind `cdc_payments` was the consumer that stopped. |

## Open questions

- Why `cdc_payments` stopped advancing at all. The incident record does not say, and the first place to ask is the owner of the reconciliation pipeline that reads it.
- How long the slot had been inactive before the alert fired. The alert measures retained size, not inactivity, so those two moments may differ.
- Whether the slot was kept or dropped at the end. Nothing in the record states either.

## Follow-ups

- Tell the pipeline owner when the slot first goes inactive, not only when retained WAL reaches 50 GB.
- Write down in the ticket how the slot was released, since this one did not record it.
