# fraud-scorer-02 throttling, 22 August 2026: what people asked

Collected afterwards from the questions people sent to the risk rota. Severity sev3, 14:02 to 15:22 UTC. Runbooks: RB-002, RB-006.

### What did payments see?

From 14:02, payments-api-01.prod.internal logged `PAYAPI-W0310`: a fraud score did not arrive in time, so the payment went through the static rules instead of the model. Our pager went off at 14:09.

### Why was the scorer slow if its CPU graph was fine?

An average hides bursts. At 14:20 we flipped Grafana's "Container CPU" to throttled periods, and fraud-scorer-02 sat above the 25% mark. In busy stretches the pod burned through its CFS allowance early and then stalled until its next slice. Reading the cgroup counters by hand twice gave the same answer: throttled slices grew in step with total slices.

### Was it the worker-count problem from last year?

No. The older trouble was gunicorn counting the host's cores. Since late May the Deployment pins `WEB_CONCURRENCY=16`, and listing the master's children on fraud-scorer-02 showed 16 processes. Whether the pod lands on a 16-core host or a 32-core one, it gets 16. The count was right; the allowance was simply smaller than what 16 busy processes want during a spike.

### Could it have been memory growth instead?

RB-006 came first, since swelling memory also makes scoring slow. fraud-scorer-02's working set was level, it had not restarted, and feature reads were quick. No sign of cache growth.

### What changed to cause it today?

We do not know yet. The throttling started with the afternoon load. Why that load produced bigger bursts than usual is still open.

### What was the fix?

At 14:48 we gave the Deployment a larger CPU limit and kept its request as it was, so placement stayed put. New pods were all serving by 15:01. At 15:08 fraud-scorer-02 was back below 25% throttled, and payments-api-01 had gone quiet. Closed at 15:22 after a watch.

### Why not add replicas?

RB-002 warns against it. Extra replicas carry the identical 16 processes and the identical cap, so they would be throttled too.

### Why not lower the worker count instead?

It was on the table. Fewer processes would fit the cap, but each pod would handle fewer scores in parallel, and that trade deserves a calmer day than a live page.

### What is still open?

- Find out what made this afternoon's bursts bigger.
- Decide whether the higher cap stays, or whether 16 processes is more than one pod needs.
- Page on throttled share for fraud-scorer, so a calm-looking average never stands alone.
