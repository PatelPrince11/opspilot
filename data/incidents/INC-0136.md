# A quiet pool, then a full one: ledger-service-02 in the small hours

The incident ran from 04:40 to 05:20 UTC on 8 March 2026, forty minutes, rated sev3. It touched one ledger-service instance, ledger-service-02, and the database it talks to, ledger-db-01. This is the story as it can be told from the metrics and the logs.

It was a small-hours stretch on a Sunday, not the night of a month-end batch. RB-013 is the runbook for this Hikari pool, and it was written about a different cause.

At 04:40 `LEDGER-W210` appeared for ledger-service-02: more than ninety percent of its Hikari pool was in use. With 20 connections in the pool, that means 18 or more were checked out. Within moments none were left. From then on a caller needing the database queued, and Hikari lets a queued caller wait thirty seconds before it gives up. Each caller that reached the limit left one error line saying the request timed out after 30000ms. On the other side of the call, payments-api was getting `503` back from ledger-service.

Why would a pool of 20 stay full? Either the server hands out no more connections, or the ones in use are not coming back. The first did not apply. RB-012 holds the connection budget for ledger-db: its two ledger-service pods can hold 40 connections between them, against the 200 the database allows. The pool had its connections; they were simply occupied. On ledger-db-01, sessions from the ledger application login were working slowly through a shared statement, and every connection they sat on was one fewer for callers.

Which statement that was, and the reason it ran slowly, is not known. Nobody copied it out while it was running, so the story ends at the sessions.

The ledger on-call took the incident. By 05:20 the pool was emptying, the warnings were gone and payments-api no longer saw `503` from ledger-service. What ended the slow work is not in the record.

The incident left one practical lesson, and it is about evidence rather than settings: when the pool fills again, copy the running statements from the database into the ticket before anything else changes.
