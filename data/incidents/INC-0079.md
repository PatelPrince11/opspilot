# Night to day, Tuesday 16 September 2025

**redis-cache-02 · /var climbing past 90% · sev4 · 03:35 to 04:35 UTC · RB-008 · closed**

TL;DR: the journal on redis-cache-02 was eating the root disk. Fixed on that one VM, without touching Redis.

## What happened on my shift

- 03:35 · doing a routine pass over the cache hosts' dashboards, I saw redis-cache-02's root disk graph sitting above 90% and still sloping upward.
- 03:41 · before touching the disk, made sure the box was healthy. `redis-cli PING` on the cache port answered `PONG`. This host also runs one of the three Sentinels, so I asked it who the primary of `cache` is; it replied straight away. Neither process had complained.
- 03:48 · looked for what was big on `/var`. My first guess was Redis's own files, since this is a datastore. Wrong: they were small. The bulk was `/var/log/journal`.
- 03:52 · `journalctl --disk-usage` confirmed it.
- 03:58 · `sudo journalctl --vacuum-size=500M`. Usage on `/var` dropped well clear of the threshold.
- 04:06 · added `SystemMaxUse=500M` under `[Journal]`, restarted `systemd-journald`, checked `--disk-usage` again: inside the limit.
- 04:35 · nothing growing for half an hour, closed. 60 minutes start to finish.
