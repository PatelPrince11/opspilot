# The 23 August 2025 slow feature store, asked and answered

sev3 · 03:35 to 04:00 UTC · store: redis-features-01 · seen in: fraud-scorer-01, payments-api · runbook: RB-006

**What showed up first?**

At 03:35 fraud-scorer-01 started logging `FRD-W118`, the warning for slow feature reads from the store (RB-006 pairs it with `PAYAPI-W0310` on the payments side). Soon after, payments-api logged `PAYAPI-W0310`. Both kept appearing until 04:00.

**Two services complain. Where is the fault?**

On redis-features-01. The feature store is one machine with nothing to fail over to, so when it drags, every reader drags with it. The scorer and payments-api are only where the delay becomes visible.

**What made the store slow?**

Key scans. One of them can occupy Redis's single serving thread for a while, and ordinary lookups line up behind it. RB-006 names big scans among the usual reasons for this store to slow down; a background save is also on its list, and nothing here points to one.

**Why did the scans matter to payments?**

Payments-api consumes the scorer's output, and the scorer consumes the store's. A delay at the bottom of that chain reaches the top as a warning from each service in turn, which is the order in which the two codes appeared.

**Who ran the scans?**

Not known. The client and the purpose were not recorded.

**Was it the memory problem RB-006 is titled after?**

That runbook is about memory creep in the scorer, and it treats a slow feature store as what feeds the creep. It is cited for the link between the store and `FRD-W118`. Whether memory in the scorer moved during these minutes was not recorded.

**How big was it?**

From 03:35 to 04:00 is 25 minutes, rated sev3. How many payments were touched by `PAYAPI-W0310` was not counted.

**When was it over?**

The risk on-call took the incident, and the warnings were gone by 04:00. What stopped the scans was not recorded.

**What should change?**

- Learn which client issues the scans, and have it page through keys in small steps or run off this node.
- Put the store's latency on the first dashboard the risk on-call opens for a `PAYAPI-W0310` page.
- Keep the scorer's `FRD-W118` count beside it.
