# #platform-oncall: payments-api pools after the release, 23 September 2025

Thread digest. sev2, UTC, 11:00 to 11:30, so thirty minutes. Hosts in the thread: payments-api-01 and payments-api-03. Runbooks in play: RB-010 and RB-014.

**Opening, 11:00.** Payment requests on payments-api-01 and payments-api-03 started failing with `503`, and both pods logged `PAYAPI-E1042`: requests gave up after 500 ms waiting for a connection from the pgx pool. The change in play was a payments-api release.

**What the pools showed.** The pools on both pods filled to their 40-connection ceiling and stayed there; the `PAYAPI-E1042` lines came once they were full. The other two pods of the four were not part of the discussion, so the digest says nothing about them.

**Scope.** Four pods normally serve payments, each with up to 40 pooled connections, so 160 in all. Two full pools put 80 of those out of reach for thirty minutes. No failure count or customer figure came up in the thread.

**Which runbook.** RB-010 covers a full pgx pool, and it lists a pool that fills right after a deploy as a sign of new code that does not return its connection; the runbook's answer for that case is a rollback. RB-014 was opened for the gateway side and sent payment routes to team-payments, so the thread stayed with the service. Neither runbook's advice is a record of what was done here.

**What the release changed, and whether it was rolled back.** Neither is recorded.

**11:30, close.** The payments on-call took the incident, and by 11:30 `PAYAPI-E1042` was gone and the `503` responses with it. The thread states no action that ended it, so what brought the pools back is open.
