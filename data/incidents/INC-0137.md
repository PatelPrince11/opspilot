# Search refused at the edge on 10 March 2026

I was on call for this sev3, 09:05 to 09:50 UTC. The short version is that an edge setting was too small for a day it had never been asked to handle, and the fix was a bigger number plus a restart.

**The change.** Each of the three edge pods, edge-gateway-01, edge-gateway-02 and edge-gateway-03, now lets 250 `/search` requests a second through, up from 100. That is 3 x 250 = 750 across the edge, against the 420 a second the morning's demand reached. The value sits in the edge ConfigMap, which Envoy reads at start, so it took a restart of the edge pods to apply: the change merged at 09:42 and the restart ran from 09:46 to 09:50. Refusals ended there. From the merge to the end that is 8 of the 45 minutes the incident lasted.

**What users got.** A `429` whose body carried `EDGE-RL01`. With 100 a second allowed per pod, the edge could pass 300 a second at most, so with demand near 420 about 120 a second were turned away, which is roughly 29% of searches. The first refusals came at 09:05, soon after the growth team's spring campaign began sending.

**Why it took a while.** The report said search was failing, so I looked at search-api and search-cluster first. Both were fine. The cluster was healthy and search-api's rate was flat at about 300 a second. I did not at first see that the flat figure was a ceiling set in front of it rather than the demand. RB-014 did not help directly: it is organised around slow upstreams, and nothing here was slow. What found it was filtering the edge's own access logs for `/search` and looking at the answers: `429`, on all three pods, in similar numbers each.

**After.** team-catalog looked at the cluster before the limit was raised, and agreed that it could take more.

**What did not change.** Nothing was altered in search-api or search-cluster, and no other route was touched. The setting that moved was the `/search` limit on the edge, and only that. If the edge pod count changes later, the combined limit moves with it, so the figure should be rechecked then.
