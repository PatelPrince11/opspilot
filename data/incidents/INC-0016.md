## Why 2 replicas stopped being enough

No pod asks internal-dns anything. A pod's resolver is cluster CoreDNS, which handles Kubernetes
names itself and relays `prod.internal` questions to internal-dns. Starve those CoreDNS pods of
CPU and every namespace loses name resolution together, while VMs, which query internal-dns
without going through Kubernetes, carry on unbothered. That is what 20:00 and 20:02 showed.

Nobody autoscales coredns; someone picks its size by hand. It was still at 2. Going to 4 roughly
halves what each pod must answer; within minutes of the new pods being Ready, the errors were
fading and lookups came back clean.
