# edge-gateway-01: handshakes abandoned by one client (sev4)

Thursday 17 July 2025, 17:20 to 21:25 UTC, 245 minutes. Runbook RB-025. One client reported certificate trouble when connecting to our edge. The handshakes it abandoned were logged on edge-gateway-01, and the certificate presented there was the one examined.

The evidence pointed at the client. Other clients connected normally, whereas a bad chain or an expired certificate would fail for all of them. A device with a wrong clock misjudges a sound certificate on any pod it reaches, and RB-025 lists that situation as the client's to sort out; the edge needs no adjustment.

Nothing was altered on the edge side. The abandoned handshakes from that client had stopped by 21:25, and the report was closed as a problem at the client.

The client's identity, device and error text were not recorded, and no confirmation about its clock exists.
