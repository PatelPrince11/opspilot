# notification-worker-01: OOMKilled in the Monday batch (sev4)

Monday 23 February 2026, 07:00 to 08:10 UTC. Runbook RB-005. The batch of emails that goes out on Monday mornings began at 07:00. At 07:02 the container behind notification-worker-01 was killed with `OOMKilled` and the pod restarted. Memory on this worker is flat the rest of the week, and a leak would climb, so the pattern pointed to a limit that sits below what the batch needs at its busiest and not to a leak. Nothing numeric is recorded here about either the limit or the peak. At 07:55 the limit was raised above the peak and the pod restarted once more with the larger allowance. The batch then ran on, and the incident closed at 08:10.
