# Evening handoff, 22 May 2025: auth-service logins failing

Rated sev2. The trouble began at 21:32 UTC and was over at 21:52, so it lasted 20 minutes. Hosts: auth-service-01 and auth-service-02. Runbook opened: RB-010.

## Current state

Logins are working again. From 21:32 sign-ins slowed, and 503 responses from auth-service followed for the rest of the 20 minutes; the cause sat one layer down, in auth-db, which was slow to answer. The incident is closed and this note carries the follow-ups.

## What was going on

auth-service talks to its database through a pgx connection pool capped at 30 connections per pod. With two pods that is 2 x 30 = 60 connections in total. auth-service's own 60 fits inside auth-db's 200. The shortage was inside the service. Each login needs a connection, and while auth-db was slow every connection stayed checked out far longer than usual. New logins found the pool empty and waited behind the ones in progress.

The `503` is auth-service's own doing. The service has a ceiling: once more than 64 logins per pod are in flight at the same time, it returns `503` straight away. The first `503`s appeared when the waiting logins reached that ceiling, a few minutes after the slowdown began. It is not a message from auth-db and not a timeout from the pool; auth-service has no separate acquire timeout of its own.

## Where RB-010 fits

RB-010 is the reference card for a pgx pool running dry, written around payments-api, and it is the runbook cited for this incident. Its pool-exhaustion reading applies to auth-service; its acquire-deadline details do not, since auth-service has neither.

## What is not known

- What ended the slowness was not recorded; the `503`s stopped by 21:52.
