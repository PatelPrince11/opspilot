# Month-end reconciliation: CPU on one ledger instance

## Reading the signals

**Why the warning came first.** `LEDGER-W210` fires when the share of the connection pool in use passes 90%. RB-001 lists it as an early sign when a ledger-service instance is short of CPU: threads that compete for CPU hold their connections longer, so the pool fills as a consequence. Here the warning was that consequence, and the load itself was the serialization of the reconciliation result on a `http-nio-*` request thread, a case that shows up on the single instance that took the batch.

**What it was not.** Pool exhaustion caused by reconciliation parallelism is a different problem, and the setting behind it has been at 4 since 2026-02-01. The retry threads for Kafka publishes were not what burned the CPU either.

## Context

The reconciliation runs once a month, at month end, and this occurrence began late on 1 June. It is triggered over HTTP, which is why the request thread carried the serialization. Payments traffic through ledger-service is not described for this period.
