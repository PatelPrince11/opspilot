sev2, 11:20 to 11:35 UTC, 15 minutes. The thread opened with failed lookups on notification-worker-01 at 11:20 and closed at 11:35 with clean lookups. Messages are grouped below by who wrote them, not by clock, because no times were kept between those two. RB-020 is the runbook that came up.

**notification-worker side**

notification-worker-01 began logging failed lookups for `prod.internal` names at 11:20. The failures were intermittent: the same name resolved on some attempts and failed on others. Where it failed, the reply was SERVFAIL, which means a server could not produce an answer at all, as opposed to saying that a name does not exist. The symptom was logged on notification-worker-01.

**platform on-call**

SERVFAIL on some attempts only points at one of the two authoritative servers, not at the name. The zone had failed to load on internal-dns-01. Pods ask the cluster resolver, which passes every `prod.internal` question on to a server picked by chance, so a share of the worker's lookups landed on internal-dns-01 and got SERVFAIL while the rest went to the healthy server. Why the zone failed to load was not established.

**Background posted in the thread**

internal-dns is two VMs running CoreDNS, each holding its own copy of the `prod.internal` zone, and both are authoritative for it. Records carry a 60 s TTL.
