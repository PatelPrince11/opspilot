# Questions and answers: the early-morning lookup slowdown, 23 July 2026

sev4. 03:00 to 06:35 UTC on 23 July 2026, a span of 215 minutes. Pods affected: notification-worker-01 and notification-worker-02. Runbook: RB-021.

**What was wrong, in one line?**

Both workers resolved names slowly, and a few lookups finished in `EAI_AGAIN`, the status that means a try-again.

**Did mail stop?**

How many sends were delayed, and whether any failed, was not counted. notification-worker hands each message to an external email provider over HTTP, with 20 sends in flight per pod, so a lookup that stalls holds up the send that needs it.

**What was behind the slow lookups?**

With the cluster default of `ndots:5` in place, a short name is expanded with the cluster's suffixes first and only then looked up as given.

**Was this particular to notification-worker?**

The default belongs to the cluster; a Deployment has to override it to escape it, and the record does not show that notification-worker did.

**Were the two pods affected alike?**

Both pods show slow lookups and `EAI_AGAIN` in their logs. If one fared worse than the other, that went unwritten, as did any gap between their first slow lookups.

**How big was the effect?**

Nobody measured it: the number of slow lookups and of `EAI_AGAIN` lines was never tallied, and the traffic at a 03:00 start is not described.

**What does the service do, and what did the slowdown touch?**

notification-worker is a consumer in the group `notification-worker`, reading the `notifications.outbound` topic and sending each message out through the email provider's HTTP API. Two pods share that work. The slowdown touched the name lookups on those pods; the record names no other service as affected, and the topic and its group are not recorded as having a problem of their own.

**How long did it last?**

The incident record starts at 03:00 and ends at 06:35, which is 215 minutes. Nothing between those times is dated.

**Who dealt with it, and when did it end?**

The engineer on call handled it, and lookups were normal again at 06:35. The step that ended the problem is unrecorded.
