# Ticket: payment requests timing out at the edge, payments-api pods throttled

**Description.** Payment requests are coming back from the edge as `504`. The gateway answers `504` when the upstream has not responded within its 15 second route timeout, and the slow upstream here is payments-api. Both payments-api-01 and payments-api-02 were being throttled on CPU, and the throttling held requests past that timeout.

**Where the symptom shows.** The failing requests are payment calls, which reach payments-api through the route `/v1/payments/*`. The edge is where the error is produced and where customers meet it; the delay itself is inside the two payments-api pods.

**Reading the numbers.** RB-002 counts throttling above about 25% of periods as significant, and says the throttling view of the Grafana "Container CPU" panel is where to see it. This ticket records no throttled-period percentage and no CPU figure, so none is stated.

**Scope.** The ticket covers two hosts, payments-api-01 and payments-api-02, one family of routes, `/v1/payments/*`, and one span of time, from 18:10 to 19:15 on 23 January 2025. Throttling here means the pods were held back by their CPU limit for part of each scheduling period, so requests waited even though the pods were running. RB-002 is the runbook for that condition on payments-api and fraud-scorer.

**Why the edge is in the title.** The edge-gateway pods are not hosts of this incident. They appear because they are where a slow upstream turns into an error: a request that outlasts the route timeout is ended by the gateway, and the caller receives the `504`. Nothing is recorded about the gateway pods themselves being unwell.

**Ownership.** team-platform owns RB-002 and took the ticket. payments-api sets `GOMAXPROCS` from its CPU limit in its Deployment, which is the platform's usual guard against thread counts outrunning the limit.

**Timing.** The ticket was opened as the `504`s began, at 18:10, and closed at 19:15, once they had stopped. The 65 minutes in the table are that gap and nothing else; no figure for the worst minute or the busiest route is on file. The severity was sev2 for the whole span, and no entry changes it.
