# search-api-02: SRCH-E502 from search-cluster-01 and -03 (sev4)

Wednesday 19 February 2025, 07:00 to 07:45 UTC, 45 minutes. Runbook RB-003. From 07:00 a search sent to search-api-02 could fail with `SRCH-E502`. The failures came from the cluster side: two of the cluster's nodes, search-cluster-01 and -03, had full queues in their search pools and declined further queries, and the error reached callers by way of search-api-02.

Where the extra traffic came from is not known, and what was done during the incident is not recorded. The incident was rated sev4. By 07:45 search-api-02 had stopped logging the code, and team-catalog, who handled it, closed the incident.
