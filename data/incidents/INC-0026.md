# Slow payments traced to payments-pgbouncer-01

sev3 · 2025-03-22 · 14:15 to 15:00 UTC · cause: high_cpu · affected: payments-api · runbooks: RB-011, RB-002

| Where | UTC | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| payments-api | 14:15 | Latency on the payment routes began to rise. Requests that could not get a pooled connection within 500 ms logged `PAYAPI-E1042` and returned an error. |
| on-call | during the incident | Treated it as a latency complaint first, so RB-002 was on the table alongside RB-011. |
| payments-pgbouncer-01 | during the incident | Showed clients waiting at the same time as server connections that were not in use. That combination, plus the CPU reading on the VM, pointed at the PgBouncer process itself. |
| payments-pgbouncer-01 | during the incident | `top` showed the PgBouncer process at 100% of one core. The bouncer runs as a single process using one core, so that core was the ceiling for everything it served. |
| team-payments infra | during the incident | The problem was escalated to them. |
| payments-api | 15:00 | Connections acquired within the deadline again and `PAYAPI-E1042` was gone. Closed. |

## Scale of the exposure

payments-api runs 4 pods with a pool of 40 connections each, 160 client connections in all, and each payments-pgbouncer instance normally carries about half of them. When the one doing the work on payments-pgbouncer-01 stalls, the pods that route through it wait, hold their pooled connections, and hit the 500 ms acquire deadline. That is the path from a busy proxy to the application's error code.

## Decisions

- Pool sizes were left unchanged. Nothing in the evidence called for them, and changing a pool does not help a process that is out of CPU.
- The bouncer was escalated. What team-payments infra did next is not recorded here, only that by 15:00 the errors had stopped.

## Follow-ups

- Alert on CPU of the PgBouncer process directly, so a pinned core is visible before the application's error code appears.
- Add a line to RB-002's checklist for the case where latency has no throttling behind it and the slow component is a single-threaded proxy.
