# payments-api-03 and -04: pool full, payments failing

sev1 · 2025-04-24 · 01:55 to 02:20 UTC · cause: payments-db · affected: payments-api · runbook: RB-010

Two of the four payments-api pods could not hand out database connections for 25 minutes, and payment requests that landed on them failed.

| Where | UTC | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| payments-db-01 | from 01:55 | Queries from the payments login ran long, and each one kept its connection busy. The query text and the reason it was slow were not captured. |
| payments-api-03 | from 01:55 | Pool filling, then full at 40 of 40. New requests waited for a free connection and gave up at the 500 ms deadline. |
| payments-api-04 | from 01:55 | Same course as -03. |
| payments-api-03, payments-api-04 | after the pools filled | Each request that gave up logged `PAYAPI-E1042`, and its handler answered `503` on `/v1/payments/*`. |
| team-payments | during | Took the incident. |
| payments-api-03, payments-api-04 | 02:20 | Errors gone, pools no longer full. Closed. |

## Who was affected

Payment requests routed to payments-api-03 or payments-api-04 returned `503` on `/v1/payments/*` when they could not get a connection in time. The record holds no count of failed payments and no customer figure, and requests served by the other two pods are not described.

## Notes

**The deadline.** payments-api waits at most half a second (`DB_ACQUIRE_TIMEOUT=500ms`) for a pooled connection. Past that it logs `PAYAPI-E1042` and answers `503`. On a healthy day the 99th-percentile wait is under 50 ms, so every error here was a request that waited ten times longer than normal and still got nothing.

**Why the database.** A connection returns to the pool when its query finishes, so long-running queries on payments-db-01 keep connections out. With the pools at their 40-connection ceiling on payments-api-03 and payments-api-04, new requests had nowhere to go.

**Share of capacity.** Four pods at 40 connections each make 4 x 40 = 160 client-side connections. The two full pools, 2 x 40 = 80, were half of that.

**Runbook.** RB-010 covers pgx pool exhaustion. Neither the query behind this one nor the reason it was slow is known, and nothing explains why only these two pods filled.
