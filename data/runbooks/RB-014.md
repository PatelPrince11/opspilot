edge-gateway (Envoy, namespace `platform`; access logs stamped edge-gateway-01..03.prod.internal) is where slow requests are first visible. Every access log line carries `%RESPONSE_FLAGS%`, the total `%DURATION%`, and the upstream's own time from the `x-envoy-upstream-service-time` header. The route timeout is 15 s. Read the flag first, then the two durations, then hand off to the owning team.

| Edge shows | Flag / timing | Meaning | Next check | Hand to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `504` | `UT` | Upstream request timeout: the upstream didn't finish within the 15 s route timeout | Which cluster? Split `UT` count by `%UPSTREAM_CLUSTER%`; one cluster means one slow service | That service's team |
| `503` | `UF` | Upstream connection failure: Envoy couldn't open a connection to the pod | Pod restarts and readiness in that namespace; endpoints count | Service team |
| `503` | `UO` | Upstream overflow: Envoy's circuit breaker for that cluster (max pending or max requests) tripped | `upstream_rq_pending_overflow` on the cluster's stats; is the upstream slow, so requests pile up? | Service team, then team-platform if limits are wrong |
| `503` | `URX` | Retry limit or connect-attempt limit reached at the edge | `upstream_rq_retry` rising? The upstream is failing often enough to burn Envoy's retries | Service team |
| `503` | `UH` | No healthy upstream: every endpoint failed health checks | `kubectl -n <ns> get endpoints <service>` | Service team, urgently |
| `200`, slow | no flag; `x-envoy-upstream-service-time` ≈ `%DURATION%` | The upstream itself is slow and Envoy just waited | That service's own latency panel | Service team |
| `200`, slow | no flag; `%DURATION%` far above `x-envoy-upstream-service-time` | Time spent before or around the upstream: slow client upload, retries, or edge CPU | edge-gateway pod CPU; `upstream_rq_retry` | team-platform |
| `504` on `/search` only | `UT` | search-api slow | search-api's latency panel; rejections show up as search-api's own errors, not as an edge flag | team-catalog |
| `504` on `/v1/payments/*` | `UT` | payments-api past the route timeout | payments-api latency and error panels | team-payments |
| `504` on `/checkout` | `UT` | orders-api past the route timeout; orders-api also waits on payments-api and inventory-service | orders-api latency, then its dependencies | team-commerce |
| p99 up on every route at once | any | Shared cause: the gateway itself, internal-dns, or the node pool | edge-gateway CPU and restarts; resolver health | team-platform |

Stats come from the Envoy admin endpoint on port 9901, pod-local: `kubectl -n platform port-forward pod/<edge pod> 9901:9901` then `curl -s 'localhost:9901/stats?filter=cluster.<name>.upstream_rq'`. Close out when p99 for the affected route is back under its SLO for 15 minutes.
